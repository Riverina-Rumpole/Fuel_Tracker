# Privacy Policy for Fuel Tracker

**Last updated:** 4 July 2026

Fuel Tracker ("the app") is designed so that your data never leaves your device. This policy explains exactly what the app accesses and why.

## Summary

- The app does not have a server, does not use analytics, and does not show ads.
- Nothing you enter or photograph is transmitted anywhere. All processing happens on your device.
- No account or sign-in is required or offered.

## What the app accesses, and why

**Camera and Photo Library**
The app requests camera access so you can photograph a petrol bowser display, and photo library access so you can import a photo of one (for example, if someone else filled up your vehicle for you). Photos are used only to run on-device text recognition (OCR) to read the price, litre amount, total cost, and date shown on the display. Photos are not uploaded, shared, or sent to any server, and the app does not use them for any purpose beyond reading that fill's details.

**Vehicle and fuel fill records**
Information you enter — vehicle registrations, nicknames, odometer readings, and fill details (date, price per litre, litres, total cost) — is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. This data is used to calculate fuel efficiency metrics (km/L, L/100km, cost per km) and to generate the Excel spreadsheet described below.

**Fuel log spreadsheet**
Each vehicle's fuel log is saved as an Excel (`.xlsx`) file in the app's local document storage on your device. It is only shared if you explicitly choose to export or print it using the app's Export or Print features, which hand the file to iOS's standard share sheet or print dialog — the app itself never transmits it anywhere.

**Notifications**
If you allow it, the app schedules local reminders (for example, if you haven't logged a fill or exported a spreadsheet in a while). These reminders are generated and delivered entirely on your device; no notification service or external server is involved.

## Data sharing

The app does not share any data with third parties, because it does not transmit data anywhere in the first place. There are no analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party services integrated into the app.

## Data retention and deletion

All data is stored locally on your device for as long as you keep it in the app. You can delete individual fills or entire vehicles at any time from within the app. Deleting the app itself removes all of its locally stored data, other than any spreadsheet files you've previously exported or saved elsewhere using the Export or Print features.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children, since it does not collect information from anyone in the first place — all data stays on-device.

If this policy changes, an updated version will be posted with a new "Last updated" date above.
