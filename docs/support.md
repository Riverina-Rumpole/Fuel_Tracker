# Support for Fuel Tracker

Need help with Fuel Tracker? Start here — if your question isn't answered below, email **paulwkerr0@gmail.com** and we'll help you out.

## How it works

1. After filling up, tap **Capture Bowser Display** and frame the whole display in shot — or tap **Import their photo** if someone else filled up for you.
2. On-device OCR reads the price per litre, litres, total, and date straight into the review screen.
3. Check the details, fix anything OCR missed, then enter your current odometer reading.
4. Tap **Save to spreadsheet** — Fuel Tracker updates your fuel log and works out km/L, L/100km, and cost per km.

## Frequently asked questions

**Someone else filled up my vehicle — do I need to be there with my phone?**
No. On the capture screen, tap "Import their photo" to pick a photo from your library instead of using the camera, and it's read exactly the same way.

**OCR got a field wrong — how do I fix it?**
Every field on the review screen is editable. Tap in and correct it before saving — nothing is locked to what was scanned.

**Can I track more than one vehicle?**
Yes, up to 4. Add each one in the Vehicles tab with its own registration and optional nickname. Tap a vehicle to make it active; long-press to remove it.

**Where's my spreadsheet, and how do I get it off my phone?**
Each vehicle has its own Excel file, named after its registration. Open History and tap Export XLSX (or Print) to share it via the standard iOS share sheet or AirPrint.

**I made a mistake after saving a fill — can I fix it?**
Long-press any entry in History to delete it. The spreadsheet is regenerated automatically.

**What do km/L, L/100km, and cost/km actually mean?**
km/L and L/100km measure fuel efficiency (higher km/L is better, lower L/100km is better). Cost/km shows what each kilometre actually cost you, based on the fill before it.

**Does Fuel Tracker back up my data anywhere?**
No — everything is stored only on your device, with no account or cloud sync. If you want a backup, export each vehicle's spreadsheet (History → Export XLSX) and save it somewhere of your choosing, such as Files or email.

**Does Fuel Tracker collect any of my data?**
No. There's no server, no analytics, and no third-party services. See the [Privacy Policy](./index.md) for full details.
